Seaglass

Widgets, glass, app search, notification badges and a richer task switcher for the Sailfish OS home screen.

Every part is a setting. If Seaglass cannot load, you get the stock home screen.

Download {{version}} How to install

For Sailfish OS {{compatible}} with Patchmanager 3.2 or newer.

Data widgets

Live information as glass blocks among your icons: a clock with the next alarm, the battery and your connections, what is playing, your next events and the weather.

Add them in the edit mode: Add widget opens a gallery with live previews. Carry one out and let it go where you want it.

Move them like icons

A long press picks a widget up anywhere on it. The icons flow around it as it moves, and it takes its place in the grid's order when you let go.

Any size that fits

The pill on a widget in the edit mode changes its size: a card of two by two cells, a shelf across the screen, or a panel of two rows.

Glass over your ambience

The app grid rises over the home screen's wallpaper, sharp or frosted like glass. The wallpaper eases back and follows the pages more slowly than the icons, and another ambience's picture can take its place. Folders, open folders and widgets are glass too.

Folders you can see into

A folder shows small icons of its apps instead of a number. It opens from its icon with a motion of your choice, zoom, spring, reveal or slide, and closes back into it.

Folders as widgets

Open a folder out into a card, a shelf or a panel on the grid, and its apps start with one tap. It stays an ordinary folder underneath.

Find any app as you type

A glass pill on the first page holds the apps you use most. Tap it for every app from A to Z with a letter rail, or start typing: accents don't matter, and initials and folder names match too.

Badges where they help

Apps with notifications show how many on their icons, folders add theirs up, and a long press on an app or a folder shows the notifications, to open or clear them.

A task switcher with names

Covers get a chip with their app's name, and recently closed apps are one tap away. Pinch to switch between the grid of covers and a row of cards.

Pull a card up to close it

In the card row, the last app you used is in front. Pull a card up and the switcher shows the point of closing; let go before it and the card springs back.

Install

  1. Get Patchmanager

    Seaglass is applied by Patchmanager 3.2 or newer. Install it from OpenRepos or Chum if you don't have it.

  2. Install the package

    Download the RPM, then install it in a terminal:

    devel-su pkcon install-local seaglass-{{version}}-1.noarch.rpm

  3. Activate Seaglass

    Open Settings, then Patchmanager, activate Seaglass, and let Patchmanager restart the home screen. Tap Seaglass there for its settings.

To take it back, deactivate it in Patchmanager or run devel-su rpm -e seaglass. More in the install guide and troubleshooting.

Screens

Safe to try

Seaglass never changes the home screen's files. Patchmanager adds a few lines that load our QML, and wherever it does not load, that part of the home screen stays stock. Removing the package takes the patch out first.

How it works

Nothing leaves the phone

Seaglass reads only what the home screen and its Events view already read. It sends nothing anywhere, keeps its settings and launch counts on the phone, and writes nothing to the system journal unless you turn on the debug log.
